Ghrelin, Withdrawal, and Real Weight Loss: A Fast Q&A

No monologue tonight. Somebody sat across from me and asked the questions people actually ask, and I answered them straight, one at a time. Twelve rounds. Here they are in writing.

I want the version that does not hurt.

So did I. For 20 years. It's a wonderful product, and it's not a plan.

Is being hungry a sign it's not working?

It's the only sign that it is. A deficit means your body went and took the difference off of you. Ghrelin rises when you eat less. Everybody agrees on that. The industry just decided the rise itself was the problem. The hunger hormone goes up in a deficit. That's plumbing. That's not a character flaw.

So the shakes and the drugs are a scam?

No, they do exactly what they promised, which is help. The problem is you don't feel the one signal that says it's working.

The refrigerator at 9:30 is real hunger, though.

You ate at 6:30 and you were full for three hours. That is not a caloric need. It's a withdrawal wearing a nametag.

How long does it last?

About 11 minutes. Nobody advertises 11 minutes, because 11 minutes isn't actually a product.

What do I do during it?

Say it out loud. This is withdrawal. This is not hunger. Then water, then bed. That's the whole skill.

Why does everybody quit at three weeks?

Because that's exactly when it gets loud, and exactly when somebody is standing there holding a product. The industry spent 30 years learning how to keep you coming back. The gentle version had 20 years to work on me. You got yourself in here. You get yourself out.

So whose fault is it? That's a little harsh. What's the actual protocol?

Beef, chicken, eggs, fish, vegetables. Short enough to say out loud and actually remember. If it has an ingredient list, you don't need it. Nobody has ever negotiated a nutrition label onto a piece of broccoli.

That's the entire thing? Sounds miserable.

A hard, seared steak with salt. A filet of fish cooked in real butter. Point to the misery.

So what are day one, day three like?

Headache. Irritable. Feels like failure. That's the substance leaving you, not the food failing.

How much did you lose? That's the impressive part.

134 pounds. 365 down to 231, under 8 months. No surgery, no Ozempic, no coach. It's arithmetic, not the achievement. The achievement was the first night I didn't open that cabinet.

Is it too late for me? Should I stop my blood pressure medication?

Fatty liver reverses in weeks. Blood pressure moves inside a month. Not yet doesn't mean not the same as never. No, I am not a doctor. Ask for a baseline panel and a 90-day trial, a retest, and let them taper you off with your own physician.

What this actually is

Everything above is arithmetic and mechanism, not a promise. A deficit is real, ghrelin is real, and the industry that sells you the feeling of avoiding both is real too. None of that changes who has to open or not open the cabinet tonight.
