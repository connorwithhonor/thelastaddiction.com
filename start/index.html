The story behind the project

A pattern that started early.

When my mom tried to lose weight in the late 1970s, our household switched to low-fat foods and margarine. We drank Coca-Cola believing it was safe because it didn’t contain fat.

I watched her struggle. As a young boy, I started gaining weight too. I carried that physical and mental weight for over 45 years.

What did I learn about food that I have never stopped to question?

That is where this conversation starts. My experience is personal. Your circumstances and the support you need are your own.
