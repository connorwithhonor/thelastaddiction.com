The hard truth

You can't just stop eating.

Every other addiction has a simple, brutal, hard, but simple solution. Stop doing the thing.

Alcohol? Stop drinking. Cigarettes? Stop smoking. The world builds systems around that abstinence.

Food is the one addiction where you cannot simply abstain. You have to face your demon three times a day. Every single day. For the rest of your life.

Nobody talks about that. The Last Addiction does.

The question nobody asks

Are you a food addict?

Most people never ask. They blame their metabolism, their stress, their schedule. They buy the next program, try the next diet, and fail again.

If you have been trying for years, or decades, and keep failing, you may be fighting an addiction with willpower tools. That never works.

  • You think about food constantly, even right after eating.
  • You eat in secret. You hide what you bought.
  • You've lost weight before, and gained it all back, plus more.
  • You use food to cope with stress, loneliness, boredom, or emotion.
  • You know what you should eat, and you eat the wrong thing anyway.
  • You've been starting Monday for years.

If any of those hit, you're in the right place.
